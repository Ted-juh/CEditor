1 · An audio effect in the dock: its three CTRL49 pages

Each audio block in the chain now shows its dry/wet, what it is doing to the sound, its latency and its state. Click one and its pages open underneath: EFFECTS, the whole chain measured; EFFECT, this plug-in's own parameters on the eight knobs; SECTION, a section of its own window, where the panel scan has done it. The same three drawings are the keyboard's pages.

2 · The three pages at the keyboard's own size

480 × 272, in the original look. On EFFECTS pad N switches effect N on and off; on EFFECT and SECTION the pads switch the effect and step through its groups or sections, and pad 8 goes back to EFFECTS.
