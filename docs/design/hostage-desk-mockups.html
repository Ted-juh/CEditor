1 · METERS grows into DESK

Left, today's METERS page: five parts and the master on strips, the knobs as faders. Right, the same page grown: eight strips and the master, played by the CTRL49's nine faders, a strip per fader. The bank above says what the eight are: the inputs, the parts, buses and returns, or the monitor mixes; Bank ◀ ▶ steps through them, eight at a time, so 32 inputs are four banks. The pads mute, solo, listen and select, one strip each; the knobs are each strip's pan.

2 · CHANNEL and MIX

Pad layer D selects a strip; CHANNEL then shows its built-in channel strip: high-pass, gate, four-band EQ and compressor, all HoSTage's own, so no isolation block. MIX turns the faders into one monitor mix (sends on faders): each fader sets how much of its strip that player hears, the ninth the mix's own level. Pick the mix on the pads; hold TALK to speak into it.

3 · The Mixer tab as the desk

The same desk on screen, from today's Mixer tab: the same banks, narrower strips so sixteen fit, each with its source, its channel strip at a glance (HP, G, EQ, C lit when on) and its gain reduction. "Faders show" flips every strip to one monitor mix, as MIX does on the keyboard. The selected strip's channel strip opens on the right.
