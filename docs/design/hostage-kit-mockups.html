1 · Playing the kit, in the dock

The drum machine gets a Pads panel: what the eight pads do now, and how they feel. Five ways of playing (play, repeat, one drum, mute, erase), one strength setting for the pads alone, and the repeat rate. Here "one drum" is on: the snare is spread over all eight pads, softest to hardest. The pattern bar gains ×2, which doubles the pattern.

2 · KIT on the CTRL49, three ways of playing

KIT keeps the pads as the drums and gains a MODE knob. REPEAT: hold a pad and it repeats at the rate, swung like the pattern. MUTE: each pad mutes or unmutes its drum while the pattern plays. ERASE: hold a pad and its drum's hits are cleared as the playhead passes them. STRENGTH is the pads' response, FULL its top. PATTERN gains ×2. Which knob and pad does what is settled when the pages are built.
