1 · Sample pads in the dock

A built-in instrument, the file player made playable: sixteen pads a bank, each one sound file, played as it is. Drop files on the pads, or a whole folder: each file on its own pad, named after the file, a kick on the kick's note when its name says so. Select a pad to trim it on its waveform. The drum machine plays it like any instrument; so does the keyboard.

2 · Two pages on the CTRL49

SAMPLES puts the bank on the pads: play them, or hold SELECT and press one to edit it. TRIM is the selected sample on its waveform: start, end and the fades on the knobs, then level, pitch and how it plays. Which knob and pad does what is settled when the pages are built.
