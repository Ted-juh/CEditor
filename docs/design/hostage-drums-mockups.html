1 · The drum machine in the dock

A drum part's chain starts with a DRUMS block: its patterns. Below it, the grid: a lane per drum, named from the instrument's own note names, sixteen steps across. Each hit shows its strength as height; a faded hit plays only sometimes; dots are repeats; a small "1:2" plays every second time round. The shaker's lane is twelve steps long and drifts against the rest. Click to place a hit, drag up or down for its strength. ▶ plays the pattern on its own while you build it.

2 · Three pages on the CTRL49

DRUMS is the grid at the keyboard's size: knob 1 picks the drum, the pads are the sixteen steps of that drum in two halves. KIT turns the pads into the drums themselves, to play them and to record into the pattern. PATTERN chooses the pattern and its variation, the groove and the length, and holds the fill. Which knob and pad does what is checked against the rest of the keyboard when the pages are built.
