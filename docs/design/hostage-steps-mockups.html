1 · Step effects in the dock

A part's chain starts with a STEPS block when its pattern moves settings in time. Each lane is one setting, stepped: the pad's own level chopped into a gate, the synth's cutoff opening every fourth beat, the delay's mix thrown on the last steps of a twelve-step lane that drifts against the rest. The blocks the lanes touch say so. Add a lane by picking a setting, or by moving it.

2 · STEPS on the CTRL49

One page for every stepped setting of the part: knob 1 picks the lane, knob 2 the step, then its value; glide, the fill shape, the range and the lane's length follow. The pads are the steps, a half at a time: for a gate, on and off; for any other lane, a press sets the step to the lane's top or back to its bottom.

What a lane can move

  • The part's own level (a gate), its pan and its sends: HoSTage's own, ramped, never clicking.
  • Any setting of its instrument or effects, by name, as the Controller tab lists them.
  • A macro, so one lane moves several settings at once.

Where the line is

  • Inside the pattern's loop, up to 64 steps (features record, "Where the line is"). Never automation drawn across a whole song.
