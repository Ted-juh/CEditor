1 · The Chords module in the dock: its six CTRL49 pages, side by side

The module as it is today, one page per part of it: CHORDS to play from, SET for the chords themselves, then a page for each layer that plays them: FOLLOW (every key plays a shape), KEYS (chosen keys play chosen chords), PADS (eight pads, four banks) and PROG (a progression stepped by key or pedal). Each page is the keyboard's own, drawn once and shown in both places.

2 · The six pages at the keyboard's own size

480 × 272, in the original look. Knob N is cell N on every page; on PADS, knob N also chooses pad N's chord. Shift and the pad bank buttons choose the bank, as they do now.
