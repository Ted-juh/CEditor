1 · Three choices of skin, each its own

In the Utilities drawer, one page with three rows. Each row offers the fifteen: the fourteen CTRL49 skins and HoSTage's own look. The contrast check runs on what you pick and names any pair that would be hard to read. The Stage screen's ☀ (high contrast for bright rooms) stays, as a one-tap override over whatever Stage skin is chosen.

The HoSTage skin is Walnut: the window, the rack, the browser and the dock's frame take Walnut's colours through HoSTage's own tokens. The CTRL49 skin is Midnight: the page in the dock is drawn exactly as the keyboard shows it. Part colours stay HoSTage's six; "Missing" keeps HoSTage's red.

Left, the Stage skin left at "same as HoSTage" with HoSTage's own look.
