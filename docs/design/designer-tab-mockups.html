The Designer tab

Fourteen components have content that is a drawing. None of it can be authored: the properties panel has no drawing surface, and preview mode throws away what you draw in it.

Built on 2026-09-10, three designers of fourteen. The candidate list called this "the largest piece of work here" and said to prove the pattern on one component first. Three prove it better than one, because they are three different shapes. The eleven that are not built are named on screen rather than silently absent.
Plate 1

Whatever you draw in preview is thrown away

Several of these components can be drawn on — in preview mode. And stores/previewRehearsal.js photographs the panels store when preview starts and puts it back when preview stops. That is deliberate and right: before it, a runtime script's set() edited the author's file and autosaved it, with no undo step they would recognise. The bracket fixed that.

What it also means is that every drawing route in the application today is inside that bracket. Measured, driving the real stores:

before preview        : 8 cells      (a Phrase ships with a starter pattern)
drawn during preview  : 2 cells
after leaving preview : 8 cells

Preview also turns off selection, dragging, the context menu and the marquee, so it is not a mode you design in anyway. It is a mode you enter, and leave.

Plate 2

And the Step Sequencer cannot be drawn even there

utils/stepSequencerLayout.js exports 24 things. Seven are never imported anywhere, and five of those seven are exactly the editing half:

ExportWhat it doesImported by
cellAtPointwhich cell is under the pointernothing
cellAtread one cellnothing
isCellOnis it litnothing
toggleCellturn one on or offnothing
setCellVelocityset its velocitynothing

The default pattern is {}. The properties panel section edits steps, tempo, direction, tracks and colours and never the pattern. The preview surface imports the sequencer's clock half — advanceStep, stepMs, stepNotes — and none of its editing half.

So a Step Sequencer draws an empty grid and plays silence. In the editor, in preview, and in the exported player. It has done since it shipped.

There is a third thing in that. The renderer draws each lit cell's opacity from its velocity — so the picture has always had a dimension the data could not carry, because nothing could write one.

This tab does not take a single row out of the panel, because what it adds is the part the panel does not have. The space and the defect point at different things here, and the defect is the one worth building for.

Decided while building

  • One tab, many editors — a registry, not a switch. The registry lists all fourteen with the section they edit and the shape their content is. Adding a designer is a row and an import. rack-canvas-plan.md proposes the same mechanism for the instrument host; if both are built they should be one thing.
  • The designer is the renderer. What you draw on is the component itself, at dock size, not a sketch of it that is free to disagree — the rule EffectPreview already settled here.
  • The Step Sequencer went first even though its section is the third shortest. The height was never the reason.
  • moveZoneEdge looked dead and is not. A first pass for unused exports flagged it; it is called by dragSplitPoint in the same file, which the preview surface calls. Checked before it went into a document. The same pass flagged phraseLayout.clearPattern, which really is dead.
  • Nothing was removed. All fourteen section editors still draw every row they drew.
