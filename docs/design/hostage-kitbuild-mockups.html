1 · A kit from anywhere

Each drum of the kit chooses its own source: a sound in Samples, a drum of a plug-in, or a note on a hardware drum machine. The grid plays them as one kit. A closed hat from Samples still cuts the open hat from the plug-in (one choke group across them all). The hardware drum machine answers late, so its hits are sent early by what was measured; plug-ins need nothing, HoSTage already lines them up.

2 · Split a kit onto mixer strips

The drum plug-in has several outputs. "Split onto strips" finds them by their own names and makes each a full strip: its own effects, sends, pan, mute and solo, all into the "Drums" bus with the kit's other sources. Today an extra output only gets a level into the master; here it becomes a strip like any part's.
