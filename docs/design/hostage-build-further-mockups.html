1 · Knobs follow the selection

In Build, select a part and the CTRL49 goes to that part's INSTRUMENT page (PARAMS for a hardware synth): the eight knobs play its main parameters at once, chosen by the page's own rules. No page to make. Pages stay for the show: on the Stage screen, and in Performance, the knobs do what the song's page says.

2 · A macro by moving

Press Learn macro, then turn the knobs to where the macro's far end should take them, here, in the plug-in's own window or on the controller, on any part. Each one caught is listed from where it was to where it went. Press Done: one macro now moves all of them together, from there to there, and can be put on a knob like any other.

3 · Swap a plug-in, keep the mappings

Moog Bass moves from Diva to Mini V. Everything that pointed at Diva's parameters (knob pages, macros, modulation, the INSTRUMENT page's pins) is offered Mini V's parameter of the same or a similar name. Sure matches are ticked; a guess asks; one with nothing near it says so. Today all of them would become unresolved.

4 · Find a sound from a recording

Drop a few seconds of audio on the Sounds tab: a demo, a song, a voice memo of a synth. Mark the stretch where the sound plays on its own; HoSTage measures it the way the library measures every sound and lists the ones you own that are nearest, with what agrees and what does not. Play the recording and a sound one after the other, then load it.
