1 · A plug-in instrument in the dock: four pages

SOUND is the CTRL49's browser, the library of this instrument's sounds; CHANGES is the existing page that sets the sound against its save; INSTRUMENT is its parameters, chosen the way the EFFECT page chooses them; SECTION is its own window, once the panel scan is proven. The same four drawings are the keyboard's pages.

2 · A hardware synth in the dock: two pages, and its CEditor panel

PATCH is what the synth is playing: program, the captured patch, and whether it arrived, read back where the synth allows. PARAMS is its parameters from its CEditor device profile, a group at a time, written the way the profile formats them. The panel itself opens like a plug-in's window.

3 · Out in the dock: the strip, and the rig's meters

STRIP is the part's mixer strip on the keyboard's knobs: level, pan, its sends and its output; the pads are On, Mute, Solo and Listen. Beside it, the existing METERS page with every part, so the strip is never on its own.
