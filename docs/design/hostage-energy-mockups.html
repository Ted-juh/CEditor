1 · The energy knob, in the dock

One knob on the DRUMS block. In the middle, the pattern as written. Turned left, hits leave in a fixed order, the softest and the off-beat ones first, until only the backbone is left. Turned right, the hits of a fuller pattern you choose come in, the strongest first. Each position is always the same pattern, so a build can be rehearsed.

2 · Energy over the song

A section can set each pattern's energy and let it rise, so the pre-chorus builds on its own. Scenes already move settings over up to 32 beats; energy is one more thing they move, and a setting can rise with it, the pad's filter opening as the drums fill in.

The pre-chorus starts thinner than the verse, so the build has somewhere to go, and is almost the chorus by its last bar.
A rise is the knob turning itself: touch the knob and you take over, until the next section.
8 bars of 4/4 is 32 beats, the longest a scene moves a setting today (Scene::morphBeats). A scene moves a setting from where it is; starting the rise from a value of its own, −30, is new.

3 · On the CTRL49

ENERGY, reached from PATTERN: the pattern as it plays now, the knob's position, and the pads for a live build. Which knob and pad does what is settled when the page is built.

DROP takes it straight to the backbone while held: a breakdown, back on release.
RISE turns the knob for you, from where it is to RISE TO over the bars set, starting on the next bar.
AS WRITTEN goes back to the middle. KEEP makes a pattern of what is playing.
Knob 1 is the energy itself; the encoders are endless, so the screen shows where it is, and it stops at the middle on the way through.
