1 · One page, fourteen skins

The arpeggiator's PLAY page, drawn once, coloured fourteen ways. Nothing moves between skins: the same layout, the same words, the same knob under the same cell. What changes is a palette of sixteen colours, a corner radius, and for two skins one extra touch (Walnut's cheeks, Neo Brutal's outlines). The dock draws the same page with the same palette.

2 · What a skin is: sixteen colours

Each role is used the same way on every page: bg the page, card the panels, raised empty tracks and off pads, text, label, dim and foot the four kinds of words, bar a value's fill, grid and grid+ the pattern lines, primary held keys, notes and the selected tab, on what sounds now, the focused cell and lit pads, loud accents, warn the cursor and warnings, and the two key colours of a keyboard drawn on the page. Words on a filled colour take the bg or the text colour, whichever reads.
