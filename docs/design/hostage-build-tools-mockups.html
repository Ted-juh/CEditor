1 · The command palette

Ctrl+K anywhere in Build. Type what you want in plain words and press Enter; the first result acts on the part you are on. It searches every command HoSTage has, plus your parts, sounds, plug-ins and songs, and fills in a key, a name or a number from what you typed. Each result shows its shortcut or the gesture that does the same, so the palette also teaches the screen. No AI: a fixed list of words, offline.

2 · Undo that covers everything

Loading a plug-in, a sound or a preset is now a step like any other, and so is what you change in a plug-in's own window: one step, named after what changed, when you leave it. History lists the steps in words; click one to go back to it, and the steps after it stay until you do something new. A hundred steps.

3 · Drag to modulate

The modulation sources sit beside the knobs. Drag one onto any knob: it outlines every knob it can reach, and the one under the pointer says what it will do. Let go and the knob gets a coloured ring from its value to where the modulation takes it; drag on the ring to set the depth, double-click it to remove. The Matrix stays, as the list of everything connected.

4 · Hold to compare

Press Mark when the part sounds the way you want to keep for reference. Then, while editing, hold the button or the ` key to hear the part as it was at the Mark; let go to come back. The dock turns amber and every knob shows the value it had then, with today's value struck through beside it. Nothing is changed by comparing. Until a Mark is set, the button says "Mark first".
