1 · The line machine in the dock

A bass part's chain starts with a LINE block: its patterns, as a drum part's starts with DRUMS. Below it, the line on a piano roll that shows only the notes of the key, so every row is a right note. Under the roll, two rows of switches: accent (played harder, and if you like, brighter) and slide (the note runs into the next, and the synth glides). Hold a key to move the whole line from it.

2 · LINE on the CTRL49

One new page; the other two are already designed. LINE is the line at the keyboard's size, with the accents and slides marked; knob 1 walks the steps, the next ones set the step's note, accent, slide and length; the pads turn steps on and off, a half at a time. The melody's piano roll is the step sequencer's NOTES page, and choosing the pattern is the drum machine's PATTERN page.

Three pages, one new

  • LINE (new): the line, accents and slides. The pads have three layers: A the steps on and off, B each step's accent, C its slide, one press each, a half at a time.
  • NOTES: the step sequencer's page from the Chain design, the whole keyboard on its side, for writing the melody note by note.
  • PATTERN: the drum machine's page, the same for drums and lines: which part of the song, A to D, the groove, ×2.

Not the step sequencer

  • The step sequencer is a module that plays while you hold keys, like the arpeggiator.
  • The line machine is a pattern: it plays with the song, started by its sections; a held key only moves it.

3 · Slide, accent and following, made to work on any synth

The easy versions would fail quietly: a slide on a synth that does not glide is two notes at once, an accent on a sound that ignores strength does nothing, and "follow the key" leaves open which keys and how. So the line machine checks the synth, uses the library's own measure of how a sound answers strength, and says exactly how it follows.

Slide

How the slides glide

Glide bythe synthpitch bendportamento CC
Diva is in mono, legato glide is on: slides will glide. ✓
If the synth were in poly mode, each slide would sound as two notes. HoSTage reads its mode where the plug-in names it and offers Set mono legato, one click, undoable.
Pitch bend: HoSTage bends from note to note itself, for synths without glide, within the bend range (told once, or read from the device profile). Portamento CC (5 and 65): for hardware that answers it. A slide between two of the same note is a tie: held, not started again.

Moog Bass answers strength: measured by the library, a hard note is clearly louder and brighter than a soft one. ✓
A sound that hardly answers strength gets this instead: "This sound barely changes with strength, so accents will not be heard. Raise a setting on accents?" with its likeliest settings offered (cutoff, envelope amount, level).
The measure is the library's own (how much harder velocity 100 hits than 40), already taken for every sound it has auditioned.

The keys of this part's zone (C1–B2) steer the line and do not sound themselves. Holding F moves every note by the same distance as A to F, so the line keeps its shape; let go and it stays on F until another key.
"A chord" listens to a chord held on this part, or on another (the pianist's right hand), and moves each note to the nearest note of that chord, roots to the root. The roll shows the key's notes; Chromatic shows all twelve for a passing note, marked as outside the key.
