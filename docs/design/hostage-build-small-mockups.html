1 · Set by showing, set by playing

Two settings that are numbers today become gestures. A knob's or a macro's range: move the plug-in's own control to the low end, then to the high end. A layer's switch point: play the softest note that should bring in the second sound, or the key where it should start. The numbers stay, for fine-tuning.

3 · Drag to assign in Performance

Drop a saved rack on a song to make it that song's rig; drop a control page on a song or on one of its scenes to make it the page the controller shows there. A sound dropped on a song offers to make a rig for it: the current rack with that sound on the selected part. While something is held, every song and scene that takes it is outlined.
